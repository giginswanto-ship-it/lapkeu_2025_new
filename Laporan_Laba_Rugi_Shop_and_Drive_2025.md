## 4. ANALISIS RASIO KESEHATAN KEUANGAN & NERACA BENGKEL

1. **Current Ratio (Rasio Likuiditas):**
   $$\text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Aset Lancar}}{\text{Liabilitas Lancar}} = \frac{\text{Rp 580.834.434}}{\text{Rp 225.000.000}} = \mathbf{2,58 \times}$$
   *Toko sangat likuid dan mampu melunasi seluruh kewajiban jangka pendek hingga 2,58 kali lipat.*

2. **Debt to Equity Ratio (DER):**
   $$\text{DER} = \frac{\text{Total Liabilitas}}{\text{Total Ekuitas}} = \frac{\text{Rp 225.000.000}}{\text{Rp 565.834.434}} = \mathbf{39,76\%}$$
   *Struktur permodalan sangat aman dan sehat (jauh di bawah batas risiko 100%).*

3. **Return on Equity (ROE):**
   $$\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Laba Bersih}}{\text{Modal Awal}} = \frac{\text{Rp 65.834.434}}{\text{Rp 500.000.000}} = \mathbf{13,17\% \text{ per tahun}}$$

4. **Return on Assets (ROA):**
   $$\text{ROA} = \frac{\text{Laba Bersih}}{\text{Total Aset}} = \frac{\text{Rp 65.834.434}}{\text{Rp 790.834.434}} = \mathbf{8,32\%}$$

5. **Perputaran Persediaan (Inventory Turnover):**
   $$\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{HPP}}{\text{Persediaan Rata-rata}} = \frac{\text{Rp 2.870.571.001}}{\text{Rp 320.000.000}} = \mathbf{8,97 \times}$$
   *Perputaran stok aki, oli, dan ban mencapai ~9 kali setahun (rata-rata lama barang di rak hanya 40 hari).*
